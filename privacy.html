Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026

When you book with Mar Latrice, this site keeps only what she needs to confirm your appointment. Here is what that is, who sees it and when it is deleted.

What you give us

When you send a booking request or tap the deposit button, the site saves:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The service, add-ons, day, time, how long it takes, the estimated total and the deposit
  • Anything you type in the notes box
  • Whether you said you paid the deposit, and how you sent the request

Your text message goes through your own phone, and your carrier's privacy rules apply to it.

Who sees it

Only Mar Latrice's account opens her booking list. Tyrone Dunn, who builds and runs this site, has access for support and repairs. Nobody sells, rents or shares your details for ads.

When a request comes in, her phone gets an alert with the day, time and service. The alert never shows your name.

Deposits

Online deposits go through the payment company named on the checkout page. Your card, Apple Pay or Google Pay details go to them, never to this site. The payment company tells the site a deposit was paid and the amount, so she sees it next to your request.

How long we keep it

Booking requests are deleted automatically 90 days after the appointment date. To fix or delete your request sooner, text (414) 935-8785.

On your device

  • Your booking in progress is saved in your browser until you close the tab, so a refresh or the trip to checkout doesn't lose it.
  • If you turn motion off, your browser remembers that choice.

This site uses no cookies, no ad trackers and no tracking pixels.

Visit counts

The site counts how many visits it gets each day and how many people open the booking section. These are totals only. Nothing in them says who you are.

Services this site runs on

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it keeps short-lived server logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Supabase stores the booking list, open times, menu and photos, on servers in the United States.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the fonts and code libraries the page uses, so they see your IP address when the page loads.
  • Instagram photo links open Instagram, and its own privacy rules apply there.

Kids

This site is for adults booking for themselves or their children. Kids should have a parent or guardian book for them.

Questions

Text (414) 935-8785 or email mariahlatricex@gmail.com. If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
