Policies|Read before you book

The Fine Print

Book+Arrive ready+Text first

  1. Online only

    01 Booking

    Book only through the booking site. No walk-ins unless advertised. For questions, text (414) 935-8785. Text only, no calls.

  2. Deposit first

    02 Deposits + payment

    Each service has its own non-refundable deposit, shown at booking. Your appointment is not secured until the deposit is paid and you receive a confirmation. The deposit applies to your final balance, and the remaining balance is due at the end of your appointment.

  3. 48 hrs

    03 Cancel or reschedule

    Give at least 48 hours’ notice. Reschedule within the notice period and your deposit transfers one time. With less than 48 hours’ notice, the deposit is forfeited and a new deposit is needed.

  4. No‑show

    04 No-call / no-show

    A no-call/no-show forfeits the deposit. Future bookings may require full prepayment, and repeated no-shows may lose booking privileges.

  5. 10 min

    05 Running late

    There is a 10-minute grace period. After that, a $15 late fee applies, and your service may be modified or canceled if there isn’t enough time. Text if you’re running late.

  6. Arrive ready

    06 Hair prep

    Arrive with your natural hair:

    • fully detangled
    • free of braids, extensions, wigs or previous styles, unless a takedown is booked
    • free of heavy oils, creams, gels, edge control and buildup

    Extra detangling, matting or buildup may cost more or change the service. Need a takedown? Book Refresh + Reset.

  7. Limit guests

    07 Guests + children

    Children are welcome for eligible services at regular pricing. There is no separate kids’ pricing. Please limit guests; non-clients shouldn’t come unless approved.

  8. Starting at

    08 Pricing

    A “+” means a starting price. Your final price depends on length, density, product, prep, desired length, specialty hair, custom color, complexity and extra time.

  9. 72 hrs

    09 Service adjustments

    Review your hair before you leave. Concerns about the original service need to come in within 72 hours. Not covered: preference changes, a different color or style, damage at home, improper maintenance, later heat or chemical services, or late reports.

  10. Text first

    10 Squeeze-ins

    Appointments outside online availability are at the stylist’s discretion and may carry a convenience fee. Text first.
